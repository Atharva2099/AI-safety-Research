## 3. Fellowships & Mentorship Programs

| Program | Format / Duration | Focus | Ideal Entry Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MATS (ML Alignment Theory Scholars)** | 8-week intensive (In-person / Remote) | Direct mentorship with top alignment researchers (Mech Interp, RLHF, Oversight) | After 1–2 completed research artifacts |
| **SPAR (Supervised Program for Alignment Research)** | Semester-long (Remote) | Mentored project matching for junior / transitioning researchers | Early Phase 2/3 of learning |
| **ARENA (Alignment Research Engineer Accelerator)** | Intensive bootcamps | Hands-on engineering (RL, Mech Interp, evals) | Technical skill-building |
| **Apart Research** | Weekend hackathons & 3-month sprint fellowships | Rapid prototyping, red-teaming, and research papers | Experimentation & quick outputs |
